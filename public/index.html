Questions

Do my clients need an account?

No. They pick a time, enter their name and email, and pay the deposit. That's it.

Where does the deposit money go?

Straight to your own Stripe account, then to your bank on Stripe's normal payout schedule.

What if a client cancels?

If they cancel before your cutoff (48 hours by default), their deposit is refunded automatically. After that, you keep it. You can also cancel from your dashboard and refund with one tap.

Does it work for hair, nails and lashes too?

Yes. Anything booked by appointment with a deposit works the same way.

What happens after the trial?

Subscribe for $19/month to keep your page taking bookings. If you don't, your page stops accepting new bookings. Nothing is deleted.
