Questions

Before you ask.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They pick a time, enter their name and email, and send the deposit. That's it.

Does Slotlock handle the money?

No. Clients pay you directly with the methods you choose. You confirm when it arrives. We never touch it and we don't take a cut.

What if someone never sends the deposit?

Their hold expires after the time you set (24 hours by default) and the slot opens up again automatically. They get an email letting them know.

What if a client cancels?

If they cancel before your cutoff, Slotlock marks their deposit as owed back and reminds you to send it. After the cutoff, you keep it. Either way, both of you know where you stand.

Can I require a patch test?

Yes. Mark a service as needing one: clients confirm they've had a patch test, and can't book sooner than 24, 48 or 72 hours out. Add a free "Patch test" service so new clients can book one.

Can clients sign a consent form?

Yes. Turn on the consent form and clients sign it on their phone before the appointment: allergies, patch test, aftercare. You get a printable record.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere. Since clients pay you directly, you can use whatever payment apps work where you are, in your own currency.
